# Inspectra Code of Conduct

All contributors and participants are expected to adhere to the following code of conduct.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume positive intent.
- Provide constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats or targeted abuse.
- Posting private information about individuals.
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics.

## Reporting

Report violations to kevin.gregoire@nodasys.com. Reports will be handled confidentially and may involve project maintainers and legal counsel.
